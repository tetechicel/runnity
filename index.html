About Runnity

Built for every kind of runner.

Runnity started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find people to run with? We bring Indonesia's running clubs, events and your own training plan into one place — so you spend less time searching and more time running.

  1. 1

    Beginner runners

    Find beginner-friendly groups and plan your first 3K with confidence.

  2. 2

    Casual runners

    Join social runs and fun events that fit around work and family.

  3. 3

    Experienced runners

    Track consistency, chase streaks and sign up for your next race.
